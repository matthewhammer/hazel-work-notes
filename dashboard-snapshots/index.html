Skip the five stages for a term that is already final, when nothing is probed and nothing reused. ^report(29): every term the evaluator visits, values included, goes through eval_5 (state merge), eval_4 (reuse check), eval_3 (probe sample), eval_2/eval_1 (effects, state update, reuse map) before the rule; on a loop the rule is 7% of the time, garbage collection 36%, the stages and their trampolines 37%, and about a third of the passes on a loop are 'no step' visits to finals (5,002 of 14,009 for count(1000)). A fast path in evaluate: when the evaluation has no probe targets and no previous run to reuse (the library's calls, a game's rules: EvalInfo empty, IncrEval empty) and the term is an atom, a constructor, a closure or a list of finals, return it before the stages.
