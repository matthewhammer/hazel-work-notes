#45: what the page keeps, an audit

2026-10-14, Matthew and Claude. Ticket #45 on the dashboard; the series issue it feeds is #53. Branch measured: #2597 at c4e411f9c2. The question issue 005 left open ("what memory these take has not been measured") is answered here in part.

The leak

A tab on #2597, the Full Body slide, the shirt-width slider set again and again: the live heap after two forced GCs grows about 85 MB per edit, steadily, with no plateau: 399, 482, 562, 656, 749, 830, 907 MB over six edits; +1,388 MB after 15. Measured in headless Chromium with precise memory info, first through chromedriver, then over the DevTools protocol directly (node's WebSocket).

How, where heap snapshots fail

  • Heap snapshots crash the tab. HeapProfiler.takeHeapSnapshot kills this page's renderer at once, at 560 MB and at 400 MB alike (snap Chromium; the crash handler's ptrace errors in its log at that second). So no retainer paths.
  • V8's sampling heap profiler instead: started after two warm-up edits, stopped after four more, keeping only what is still alive: 390 MB sampled. It says who allocated what stays, not who holds it. The development bundle names its functions and carries /*<<src/...>>*/ comments, so each frame maps to a Reason source line.
  • What stays, by who made it: ValueHash's hash memo (98 MB: a WeakMap entry lives exactly as long as its object, so this is a symptom of the objects living); the worker's replies, unmarshaled (46 MB); the program's types and statics (ConstructorMap, Typ, Statics.add, about 50 MB); the layout made through the livelit projector (Measured, via LivelitProj, 16 MB); terms (MakeTerm, Skel). Whole versions of the editor's state, not one cache's entries.

The weak tables

Every weak structure in #2597 is a JavaScript WeakMap: no OCaml Weak or Ephemeron anywhere, so js_of_ocaml's handling of those does not come into it. A WeakMap's entry lives as long as its key, which is the trouble with them: what they keep depends on what keeps their keys, and that is written nowhere.

The strong tables (every module-level store on the main thread)

Inventoried by a read-only pass over src/web, src/haz3lcore, src/language and src/util. Its headline: nothing on the ordinary editing path adds a program version per edit by its construction. Every store sized like a program holds one version and is replaced by the next. So what grows must be a table keyed by ids that change from edit to edit; the highlighted rows are those.

Chains of versions, checked and not found. No model keeps the version before it: CachedSyntax keeps the inputs it was last made from, but never an earlier CachedSyntax; Editor's old_statics are arguments, never stored; EvalResult's streaming fields are cleared when the result lands; the evaluator's state and its incremental cache refer to each other, but replay_state unties the knot, so caches do not chain.

Side findings, worth tickets of their own: the derivation-mode memo with no bound; AppStore.gc never called; StructureShareSexp without a try/finally; HazelDB opening an IndexedDB connection for every save and never closing it; an agent's tool results keeping two whole-program segments per edit in its chat.
