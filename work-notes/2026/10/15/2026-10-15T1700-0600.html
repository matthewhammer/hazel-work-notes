^ticket(117): why the drag ground down

  • Since 0.0.35 (seven LFOs; every scene moving with the music), lfoBegin timed each scene animation from pic.getCurrentTime() minus the music's phase, read afresh at every draw.
  • The picture's SMIL clock moves once a frame, but the music's clock moves continuously. So the begins wobbled past their 0.05 s rounding, the scene's text changed on every draw, and mountScene rebuilt the whole scene on every move. Each slow frame widened the wobble.
  • 0.0.24's drag pause (pauseAnimations) made it worse: it stopped the picture's clock outright while the music's ran on.
  • Bisected with real drags in old releases: the witch's woods were smooth up to 0.0.34 and slow from 0.0.35. The witch's hut was slow from 0.0.38.
  • The fix: picGapNow() keeps the gap between the two clocks. It is re-measured only when the gap moves by more than PIC_GAP_SLACK (0.25 s: the music starting, stopping or seeking), and never while the picture is paused. Letting go of a drag moves the picture's clock on by the time held (setCurrentTime).
  • Measured with warm 60-move drags, music playing. 0.0.44 rebuilt the scene once per move; 0.0.45 rebuilds nothing.
    • our imagination: 20.6 s → 3.4 s
    • the witch's woods: 5.0 s → 2.3 s
    • the living room: 6.7 s → 1.6 s
    • the witch's hut: 7.8 s → 1.8 s
    • inside the tree house: 3.8 s → 1.1 s
    • every other place: 60 fps
    The sweep found all 82 pictures identical to 0.0.44.
  • Not changed: the witch's woods and hut are heavy even when idle (460 and 330 SMIL animations). They run at 10–15 fps in the software-only test browser, and did already in 0.0.32. A possible next step, not ticketed.
  • The tools are now in ~/du-shots/tools/: drag.mjs (env WARM, AFTER, COSTUME, NOPAUSE, PARTS, DIFF), idle.mjs, trace.mjs and prof.mjs.
