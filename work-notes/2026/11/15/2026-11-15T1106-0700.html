That was twelve public releases, 0.0.498 to 0.0.509, for M to try on her devices.

What was done

  • The oracles (^ticket(728), ^ticket(734), ^ticket(735), ^ticket(737), ^ticket(738), ^ticket(739), ^ticket(745)): the repository and its front page; a 🕰 History panel for each oracle, kept in the browser; the Tarot's spreads, from one card to the year ahead; the layout, with the code on the left in two columns and the reading first in one, checked at phone width; links from each card to Biddy Tarot and Labyrinthos; a divider between the columns; the I Ching's optional question, "relating to here/now" by default. It is linked from matthewhammer.org/hazel/ and the nav bar.
  • The house's rooms (^ticket(740), ^ticket(741), ^ticket(742)): the 🍽️ dining room and the 🍳 kitchen on the ground floor; the 🛏️ bedroom up a stairway; the 🧺 laundry room and the 🔥 furnace room down another. Every staircase has 13 steps, climbed sideways. Every doorway can be tapped, and every room has a way back the way you came (a node audit, tools/checks/houseways742.mjs).
  • The meal (^ticket(744), ^ticket(746), ^ticket(748), ^ticket(749)): the dining table, tapped, becomes a place setting seen from above. The main dish is a cheese sandwich, bean tacos, fried rice with tofu, or a slice of spinach and cheese pizza. There are sides (an apple, a banana, M's orange, bread, juice, milk) and desserts (a sundae, an ice cream bar, Italian ice in five flavours). Each food is eaten from one edge, with no holes. The family's diet is in the memory and in the game's agent instructions: everything is vegetarian.
  • The salad (^ticket(747), ^ticket(750), ^ticket(751)): a salad maker on the kitchen counter, also a choice at the table, with 14 vegetarian ingredients. Toss it, dress it, and eat it with a fork that follows the eaten edge.
  • Bites the same size (^ticket(752), 0.0.507): the eaten part grew with the square of its reach, so eating sped up. Each bite's reach is now solved for so that it takes an equal share of the food's area.
  • The Z-order salad (^ticket(753), ^ticket(755), 0.0.508 and 0.0.509): the salad is cut into 64 by 64 patches in Morton order, each weighted by what its disc takes. Its 32 bites are each 3.0 to 3.2% of the salad as drawn, so the eaten part is always the curve's beginning and never has a hole. It is drawn as at most 93 shapes (aligned squares, plus discs along the edge), and a tap redraws in about 6 ms.
  • The crunch (^ticket(754), 0.0.508): a bite's sound on the game's own synth. A low chomp (around MIDI note 40) with white noise in crackly grains over it, more grains for crisp foods; a sip is a slurp.
  • A check brought up to date: the 0.0.498 speed run failed four of southways233.mjs's checks. They had counted every way out of the living room, which since ^ticket(742) also has its way to the dining room. The check now looks at the way toward us only (90047e00); its first run will be with the next release.
  • A crash at 08:30, during a viewport resize and a drag in the browser pane, logged in the crash log. Since then, checks in the pane are scripts only, in a background tab, with no resizing.
